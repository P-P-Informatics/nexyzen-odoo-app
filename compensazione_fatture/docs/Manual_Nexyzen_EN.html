User Manual

Nexyzen plugin for Odoo 19 — Invoice offsetting

Automatically send unpaid invoices to Nexyzen to offset them without bank costs.

Nexyzen is a service by Camera di Compensazione · www.nexyzen.com

1. What it is and what it does

Nexyzen is the Camera di Compensazione platform that offsets the receivables you still have to collect against the invoices you have not paid yet, without bank costs. This plugin connects Odoo to Nexyzen and periodically and automatically sends the unpaid invoices:

  • customer invoices (active) — your receivables;
  • vendor bills (passive) — your payables.

Invoices are re-proposed on every cycle until they are settled: you never have to remember to resend them.

The plugin is multilingual: English, Italian, German, Slovenian, French and Spanish (see §10). The interface follows the Odoo user's language automatically.

2. Where to find it

After installation the Nexyzen app appears in Odoo's main menu, with two entries: Invoices to offset (the working list) and Settings.

For each selected invoice the list shows: type (customer/vendor), partner, number, due date, amount to offset and status (To send, Sent, Error).

3. Settings

Open Nexyzen → Settings. The configuration can always be changed. At the top there are two buttons: Test connection and Select and send now (runs the cycle immediately, useful for a test).

3.1 Selection criteria

Define which invoices to send.

  • Customer / vendor invoices: which types to include.
  • Due date (days from today): From / To range relative to the due date. Negative values = already overdue. Example: From -3650 and To 30 = all overdue plus those due within 30 days.
  • Amount range (residual): minimum and maximum of the amount still to be paid (0 in the maximum = no upper limit).
  • Limit to customers/vendors (optional): if empty, all are considered.

3.2 Sending

  • Sending mode:
    • Automatic: selected invoices are sent immediately;
    • With manual approval: a list is prepared for the user to review and confirm before sending (see §5).
  • Sending days: the weekdays on which the automatic cycle runs (Monday to Saturday; Sunday is not available).

3.3 Notifications

  • Notification email and mobile (optional): where Nexyzen sends confirmations and alerts about your sendings and offsets.
The connection to Nexyzen is pre-configured: no technical setup is required. Press Test connection at any time to check that the link works.

4. How the cycle works

Every day a scheduled Odoo action («Nexyzen: daily invoice sending») checks whether the current day is enabled among the sending days. If so it:

  1. selects the unpaid invoices matching the criteria;
  2. in Automatic mode sends them to Nexyzen straight away;
  3. in Manual approval mode prepares the list and waits for the user;
  4. records the result of each send (Sent or Error).

You can also run the cycle at any time with Select and send now.

5. Manual approval and editing the list

In Manual approval mode the selected invoices appear under Invoices to offset with status To send. The list is editable:

  • you can correct the Amount to offset (pre-filled with the residual, but editable: useful for partial offsetting);
  • you can remove the rows you do not want to send;
  • you select the rows and press Send to Nexyzen.

When finished, each row shows the updated status and the outcome message in the Result column.

6. Re-proposed until paid

An unpaid invoice is re-proposed on every cycle, even if already sent before: it stops appearing only when it is marked paid in Odoo. Nexyzen recognises resends of the same invoice (same number, date and creditor) and does not create duplicates.

7. Statuses and results

StatusMeaning
To sendInvoice selected, waiting to be sent (or approved).
SentNexyzen received the invoice correctly.
ErrorSending failed; the reason is shown in the Result column (e.g. missing VAT, connection failed).

8. Reviewing and accepting proposed compensations

When the Nexyzen clearing engine finds a cycle of receivables and payables that offset each other across several companies, it proposes a compensation to each participant. Open Nexyzen → Compensations to accept: the plugin retrieves the pending proposals and lists them.

Each proposal shows the offset amount, the receivable ceded (to whom, with which invoices) and the payable offset (to whom, with which invoices), plus the legal basis of the operation.

If Nexyzen does not yet have the assignor's full registry data, the proposal lists the missing fields (e.g. legal representative details): fill them in directly on the form before pressing Accept. The Accept button blocks the action and lists the fields still missing until they are all filled in.

Once every participant of the cycle has accepted, the compensation is complete and Nexyzen issues the credit-assignment letters (see §9).

9. Credit-assignment letters

Once a cycle is complete, Nexyzen deposits a credit-assignment letter for each participant: the document that formalises the completed compensation. Open Nexyzen → Credit-assignment letters to retrieve them.

Each letter shows the subject, issue date and retrieval date, with the full text of the communication. Use Print / Download PDF to get a copy to archive or share.

10. Multilingual

The interface is available in English, Italian, German, Slovenian, French and Spanish and follows the language set for the Odoo user (Preferences → Language). Below, the same screens in other languages.

11. Notes for going live

  • The connection to Nexyzen is pre-configured: no technical setup is needed.
  • Make sure the company and the customers/vendors have their VAT number filled in: it is the essential data for offsetting.
  • Choose the sending days and the mode (automatic or manual approval) that best fit your operations.
